Safety Tips for Opportunity Seekers

  • No Upfront Processing Fees: Legitimate government schemes never require fees transferred to personal UPI IDs or bank accounts.
  • Official Indian Domains: Genuine government portals end in .gov.in or .nic.in. Beware of unauthorized WhatsApp circulars.
  • Always Confirm Official Deadlines: When circulars convey artificial urgency or lack stated closing dates, always cross-check the official portal.
