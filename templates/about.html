Mission & Principles

Making Opportunities Understandable for Everyone

Avsar was conceived to solve a widespread problem in public education and citizen services: valuable notices buried under complex bureaucratic language.

The Problem

Why Do People Miss Legitimate Opportunities?

Every year, hundreds of government schemes, higher education scholarships, and apprenticeship vacancies go unutilized. Students, fresh graduates, and families face real obstacles:

  • Formal Jargon: Circulars run 15–30 pages in dense administrative English, obscuring basic facts like who qualifies.
  • Unclear Eligibility Rules: Complex criteria (parental income caps, domicile, marks criteria) leave applicants unsure whether they qualify.
  • Missed Deadlines & Scams: Lack of clear document checklists causes rejected applications, while unverified social media circulars demand illegitimate fees.
Our Approach

The Avsar Solution

Avsar transforms raw circulars into actionable application blueprints:

Plain-Language Summaries in English & Hindi
Evidence-Based Eligibility matched with source quotes
Interactive Action Plan Checklist and document requirements
Scam & fee warnings with safe verification steps
Methodology

How Evidence-Based Eligibility Works

We designed Avsar with strict anti-hallucination guardrails. Here is how our architecture works:

1

Verbatim Source Extraction

Whenever Gemini identifies an eligibility condition, it must cite the exact sentence from the notice as verbatim evidence. We never translate the quote so its legal meaning remains intact.

2

Profile Comparison

If you provide optional profile details (such as Class 9, age 14, income), Avsar compares each criterion to assign standard status values: matches, does_not_match, or unknown.

3

Trust Labeling

Every fact is explicitly labeled as From notice, Inferred, or Unverified. Missing information is flagged explicitly instead of guessed.

AI Limitations & Official Verification Notice

While Avsar uses state-of-the-art Google Gemini models to parse and simplify notices, it operates under clear ethical boundaries:

Informational Guidance Only

Avsar does not make legal determinations or guarantee acceptance. Only the issuing authority can officially determine eligibility.

Notice Authenticity

Avsar decodes the text you supply. It cannot independently authenticate whether a third-party PDF is an authentic government order.

Always confirm dates, official gazette numbers, and application procedures on the official issuing portal (such as scholarships.gov.in, upsconline.nic.in, or state government portals).
