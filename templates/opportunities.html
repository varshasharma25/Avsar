Explore Opportunity Categories

Understand how different public circulars, scholarships, and career programs work. Avsar analyzes notices that you paste to decode official requirements and criteria.

Notice Analysis Hub: Avsar evaluates official circulars you provide; it does not claim to host live job or scheme listings.
Education & Grants

Scholarships

Merit-cum-means awards, state fee waivers, central fellowships, and girls' education financial incentives.

What Avsar Extracts:

  • Class / Degree mark thresholds (e.g. 55% min)
  • Parental income caps (e.g. < ₹3.5 Lakhs/year)
  • Required certificates (Marksheet, Income, Domicile, Caste)
  • Application deadlines and National Scholarship Portal dates
Have a scholarship circular? Analyze Scholarship Notice
Public Welfare

Government Schemes

Direct benefit transfers (DBT), state youth allowances, agricultural tool subsidies, and subsidized housing programs.

What Avsar Extracts:

  • Domicile & age requirements
  • Specific benefit amounts and subsidy structures
  • Bank-Aadhaar linking and Jan Dhan account rules
  • Verification warnings against unofficial agent fees
Have a government scheme order? Analyze Scheme Notice
Experience & Research

Internships

Government ministry internships, research council student openings, public sector apprenticeships, and stipends.

What Avsar Extracts:

  • Degree discipline, semester, and minimum CGPA criteria
  • Monthly stipend figures and program duration
  • No-Objection Certificate (NOC) and college letter requirements
  • Selection mode (Merit shortlist vs Interview)
Have an internship notification? Analyze Internship Notice
Public Employment

Job Openings

Public service commissions, staff selection boards, municipal recruitments, and technical staff notifications.

What Avsar Extracts:

  • Age relaxation rules for reserved categories
  • Educational qualifications and required experience
  • Registration dates, exam dates, and official fee details
  • Official portal URLs for hall ticket downloads
Have a recruitment notice? Analyze Job Notice
Skill Building

Skill Development & Training

National skill development cohorts (PMKVY), MSME technical toolroom courses, digital literacy, and free certifications.

What Avsar Extracts:

  • Minimum qualification (e.g. 8th, 10th, 12th pass)
  • Training center locations and batch schedules
  • Course duration and certification authority
  • Post-training placement assistance terms
Have a skill training circular? Analyze Training Notice
